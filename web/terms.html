What this site is

fly-trader.app describes an experimental trading system that runs the FlyWire fruit-fly connectome as a trading policy for Solana memecoins, and publishes the live state of the hosted fly that trades for the $FLY vault. The site sells nothing and hosts no accounts. Its Trading and Vault pages prepare transactions and messages that your own wallet signs; nothing happens without your signature, and the site never holds your keys.

"fly-trader", "we", and "the project" refer to the contributors who publish the site and the software. The operator is the person who runs the hosted fly, funds its wallet, and holds the vault's pauser and upgrade-proposer keys.

The vault

  • Locking. You lock $FLY by approving the FlyVault contract for the exact amount and calling lock. Locked $FLY stays in the contract and starts earning at once.
  • What is paid. 100 % of the hosted fly's closed-trade profit above the operator's deposits, net of every fee, goes to lockers. Open positions count at cost, so only closed trades pay. SOL sent to the fly's wallet from an address that is not one of the operator's funding addresses counts as profit.
  • Weekly settlement. Every Monday 00:00 UTC the fly compares its realized profit with everything already paid out. If it is higher, the difference is split between lockers by time-weighted locked $FLY: the amount each address had locked multiplied by how long it was locked during the week. Rounding dust and a week with no lockers carry forward to the next settlement.
  • Losses carry forward. A week with a loss pays nothing, and the loss has to be made back before anything is paid again.
  • Withdrawals. You can request a withdrawal of any part of your locked $FLY at any time. That amount stops earning when you request it and can be withdrawn 7 days later. Until you withdraw, you can cancel the request, which locks the amount again. Requesting and withdrawing can never be paused.
  • Payouts never expire. SOL allocated to your address stays reserved in the fly's wallet until you claim it, however long that takes.
  • Upgrades and pausing. The contract can be upgraded, and its roles changed, only through a timelock with a public 8-day delay, which is longer than the 7-day withdrawal wait. The operator can pause the vault, which blocks new locks and cancellations only.
06

Claims

  • A claim is one message signed twice: once with the EVM wallet that locked the $FLY and once with the Solana wallet that should receive the SOL. Both signatures are required. Signing costs nothing and sends no transaction.
  • A valid claim pays all SOL owed to the EVM address at that moment, to the Solana wallet named in the claim. It usually arrives within a minute. The minimum claim is 0.002 SOL.
  • Check the Solana address before you sign: SOL sent to the address you signed for cannot be recalled.
