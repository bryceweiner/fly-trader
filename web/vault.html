How it works

  1. Lock

    Lock $FLY in the FlyVault contract. It takes two wallet confirmations: an approval for exactly that amount, then the lock. Locked $FLY starts earning at once.

  2. The fly trades

    The fly trades Solana memecoins from its own wallet. Its wallet, open positions, trades and every deposit are shown above as they happen.

  3. Weekly settlement

    Every Monday 00:00 UTC the fly adds up its closed-trade profit, after every fee, above what the operator deposited. Open positions count at cost, so only closed trades pay. If that total is higher than everything already paid out, the difference is split between lockers by locked $FLY × time locked during the week. A week with a loss pays nothing, and the loss has to be made back before anything is paid again. A week with no lockers carries its pot to the next one.

  4. Claim

    Connect your EVM wallet and any Solana wallet, and sign one claim with each. The fly checks both signatures and sends all the SOL owed to your EVM address to your Solana wallet, usually within a minute. What you have earned stays yours until you claim it; it never expires.

  5. Withdraw

    Request a withdrawal for any part of your locked $FLY. That part stops earning immediately and can be withdrawn 7 days later. Until then you can cancel the request, which locks it again. Withdrawals cannot be paused.

  6. Upgrades

    The contract can be upgraded only through a timelock with an 8-day public delay, longer than the 7-day withdrawal wait, so anyone who disagrees with a scheduled upgrade can leave first. Pausing blocks new locks and cancellations only.

Risks

  • The fly can lose money. Profit is not guaranteed. Weeks with losses pay nothing, and a drawdown can last many weeks.
  • Smart-contract risk. The vault and the timelock are code. Bugs, however unlikely, can lock or lose tokens.
  • Operational risk. The fly runs on one server with one hot wallet. Outages delay claims; a compromised key could lose the SOL waiting to be claimed.
  • Wallet risk. Anyone with your wallet keys can withdraw your $FLY or claim your SOL. Check what you sign: this page only ever asks you to approve the vault or the KyberSwap router, and to sign claim texts that start with this site's domain.
  • Price risk. $FLY and SOL prices move. What you are paid is SOL; its dollar value can fall.
