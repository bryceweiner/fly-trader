Privacy Policy

This policy explains what fly-trader.app does with information about its visitors, what its Trading and Vault pages send to third parties, and what the fly-trader software does with your data when you run it yourself.

Short version

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies of our own. If you connect a wallet, Reown's WalletConnect keeps session data in your browser's local storage. If you claim from the vault, our relay stores your public addresses, your signed claim and its status. Market and chain data come straight from KyberSwap, GeckoTerminal and the Robinhood Chain RPC. Fonts come from Google.

01

What we collect on this site

There is no sign-up, login, newsletter, comment, or contact form. We do not set cookies, run analytics, session recording, A/B testing or advertising scripts, or fingerprint browsers. The pages read their public data (the fly's stats, trades and settlements) from our relay at fly-trader.app/api.

The Trading page remembers your slippage setting in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your browser.

02

Vault claims and the relay

When you look up your position, the Vault page sends your EVM address to the relay to fetch what the vault owes it. When you claim, it sends your EVM address, your Solana address, and the two signatures to the relay, which stores them with the claim's status until the fly has processed and paid it, and keeps them afterwards as the record of what was paid. These are public addresses and signatures, not secrets, and the fly publishes per-address allocations and claims on the Vault page.

The relay uses your IP address only to rate-limit requests: it sits in a rate-limit counter that is deleted after an hour without requests, and it is never written to claim records.

03

Wallet connections (Reown / WalletConnect)

Connecting a wallet on the Trading or Vault page uses Reown's AppKit and the WalletConnect network. When you open the wallet list or connect, your browser talks to Reown's servers (for the wallet list, wallet icons, an encrypted relay to mobile wallets, a domain check, and a Solana RPC), which see your IP address and, once connected, your public wallet addresses. AppKit stores the session in your browser's local storage so the connection survives a reload; disconnecting or clearing site data removes it. Reown's handling of that data is governed by the Reown privacy policy. We turned AppKit's analytics, email and social logins, swaps and on-ramp features off.

04

Blockchain and market data

Your browser calls these services directly; each one sees your IP address and user agent:

  • Robinhood Chain RPC (rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com): balances, vault reads, and the transactions you sign.
  • KyberSwap (aggregator-api.kyberswap.com): swap quotes, and, when you swap, your wallet address and the amounts. See the KyberSwap privacy policy.
  • GeckoTerminal (api.geckoterminal.com): $FLY price and chart data. See the GeckoTerminal privacy policy.

Transactions on Robinhood Chain and Solana are public and permanent. Anything you sign on-chain, including locks, withdrawals, swaps and the SOL the fly pays you, is visible to anyone, forever.

05

Hosting logs

The site and the relay are served by Gandi Simple Hosting. Like any web host, Gandi may log the IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp of each request for security and operational purposes, under the Gandi privacy policy. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

06

Fonts

The pages load three typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them it sends your IP address and user agent to Google, which is governed by Google's privacy policy. Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies. If you would rather avoid this, block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; the site falls back to system fonts and remains fully usable.

07

External links

The site links to GitHub, X, ponsfamily.com, Solscan, Blockscout, FlyWire, and other third parties. Once you follow a link you are on their site and subject to their privacy practices, which we do not control and are not responsible for. The hero video and images are hosted with the site itself, not embedded from a third-party player.

08

The software runs on your machine

The fly-trader software is a program you download and run yourself. It has no telemetry, crash reporting, update check, or "phone home" of any kind directed at the project. Everything it records, including market data, your configuration, your wallet keys, your trades, and the brain's state, is written to files and a database on the computer you run it on and stays there.

When it runs, the software talks directly to services you configure with your own credentials, such as Helius, Jupiter, pump.fun, and the Solana network. Those services see your API keys, your requests, your wallet's public address, and any transactions you submit, under their own policies. The project is not a party to those connections and receives nothing from them.

09

GitHub and X

If you open an issue, comment, or star the repository on GitHub, or join or post in the project's X community, that activity is public and is handled by GitHub or X under their policies. We see the same public information anyone else does and keep no separate copy.

10

Children

The site is not directed at children and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone, children included. The software, the $FLY token and the vault are for adults only.

11

Your rights

The only records we keep about you are the public addresses, signatures and claim statuses described in section 02. Because they are the record of what the vault paid, and the same payments are on-chain anyway, we cannot delete them. Requests about hosting logs go to Gandi, about wallet sessions to Reown, and about font requests to Google. Your on-chain activity cannot be changed by anyone.

12

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything else, this page will say so before it happens, with a new effective date. Check the date at the top.

13

Contact

The project has no legal entity or mailbox. Questions about this policy may be raised by opening an issue on the GitHub repository. Issues are public; do not include personal information.
