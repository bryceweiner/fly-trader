01 · About

One brain, two chains, one minute at a time

fly-trader does not use a neural network shaped like a brain. It uses the brain: the synapse-level wiring diagram of an adult fruit fly, run on its real, signed connections, with a mushroom body that keeps learning from what every trade earns.

The brain

The wiring comes from FlyWire FAFB v783, the first complete synaptic connectome of an adult animal brain (Dorkenwald et al., 2024; annotations from Schlegel et al., 2024). Every neuron is present. Every connection between them is a real, electron-microscopy-reconstructed synapse. Neurotransmitter signs follow Shiu et al. (2024): GABA and glutamate inhibit, everything else excites. The build reproduces the MIT-licensed flybrain export edge for edge and checks it against the published counts.

How it works

Sensing. Once a minute, every graduated memecoin that traded becomes one row: on Solana the PumpSwap pools of pump.fun graduates, on Robinhood Chain the Uniswap v4 pools of Pons graduates, priced in ETH. A row holds 68 inputs: returns, order flow, volume, trade counts, liquidity, age and drawdowns over windows from one minute to three hours, the hour of day, the token's launch, its creator's record, insider selling, and what wallets with a record of winning are doing in it. A learned projection writes the row into 5,460 of the fly's sensory neurons, the ones that in a living fly carry smell, taste, touch and temperature.

The teacher. A gradient-boosted selector predicts each row's return over its hold after every cost a real trade would pay: the pool's fee, Jupiter's or the Pons hook's, the network fee and price impact. It is trained walk-forward on both chains' history and trusted only once judged: its settings are chosen on the first half of the held-out weeks, and it goes to work only if the second half made money after costs and beat random picks.

The fly. Activity flows through the connectome only where the wiring allows, and every connection keeps the sign its neurotransmitter gives it. The fly is trained once to imitate the selector, then scores each row as the selector's prediction plus a correction its mushroom body learns, which starts at zero. It buys only what the selector buys and sizes each buy the same way. Its learning can veto a buy, never add one.

Learning. When a trade's hold ends, the fly learns what the row actually made through dopamine-gated plasticity at the Kenyon cell → MBON synapses, the site of associative learning in a real fly. An outcome pathway learns from the paper book's results, a fill pathway from what the wallet's real fills added or lost, and each strategy has its own dopamine channel. Every hour the learning fly is checked against a frozen copy of itself: learning that hurts is rolled back, and a strategy that keeps failing the checks stops learning.

Acting. Each buy is a quarter of the growth-optimal (Kelly) bet for its score band, at most 10 % of the bankroll, 2 % of the pool and 0.5 SOL, and it is sold when its strategy's hold ends. Live orders always go first: sales at the minute boundary, buys the moment the fly has decided, and only then any paper trade, learning step or bookkeeping.

A rate network of leaky neurons: four message-passing steps per row, only the most active tenth of the Kenyon cells firing, as in the real mushroom body. The optic lobes (97,499 neurons) are left out: a token's tape is not light.

A second trading type for Kalshi prediction markets uses a visual fly: the optic lobes stay in, its inputs enter through the photoreceptors, and it trades through a taker arm and a maker arm, holding positions to settlement. It is still in development and ships dormant; none of it runs until you add a Kalshi key.

Training data

The fly learns from the life of memecoins after they graduate. On Solana the source is PumpAPI: its free hourly archive of every pump.fun trade since 2026-04-18 for training, and its live stream for trading, both turned into the same 1-minute rows. On Robinhood Chain it is fly-trader's own indexer of Pons launches, graduations and every Uniswap v4 swap, read from Envio HyperSync, with every quote asset priced in ETH. Labels charge every fee and the price impact a real trade would pay. Everything is kept in Postgres and Parquet, and every decision is journaled.

What we taught the fly

A connectome does not come with a trading strategy. Its teacher's strategies are fitted to the data, every threshold is chosen on one stretch of history and judged on a later one, and nothing trades until it has beaten random picks after costs.

UNIVERSE

Graduated memecoins, two chains

  • Solana: pump.fun tokens after their graduation to PumpSwap, quoted in SOL
  • Robinhood Chain: Pons launches after their graduation into Uniswap v4
  • At least 6 hours past graduation
  • A pool of at least 10 SOL (≈0.45 ETH) and 5 SOL (≈0.22 ETH) traded in the last 15 minutes
STRATEGIES

Fitted, not hand-picked

  • ev: any tradable minute whose predicted net return clears the buy line
  • capitulation: a falling minute with no insider selling
  • breakout: organic buying at the 1- or 3-hour high (the current model dropped it: it failed on the evaluation half)
  • Each has its own model, buy line and hold; a win filter and a dump veto screen every pick
THE GATE

Nothing trades unproven

  • Walk-forward: refit weekly, a purge day before every test, the last 21 days never fitted
  • Settings chosen on the first half of the test weeks, judged on the second
  • It must make money after costs, beat random picks and keep a profit factor of 1.3 or more
  • The fly must then pass a replay on each chain: positive, better than random, at most a 5 % chance its edge is zero
LEARNING

What the fly may change

  • Only its Kenyon cell → MBON synapses, by dopamine-gated plasticity
  • A learned veto can drop a selector buy; nothing can add one
  • Outcome pathway from the book's results, fill pathway from live fills minus paper
  • Hourly checks against a frozen copy; rollbacks; repeated trouble freezes that strategy's learning
SIZING

Bet what the record supports

  • A quarter of the Kelly bet for the score band the pick falls in
  • At most 10 % of the bankroll, 2 % of the pool and 0.5 SOL a buy
  • The gas reserve is never deployed
  • The fly takes the selector's seat only when its 14-day profit is at least the selector's, over 30 or more trades
RISK RAILS

Capital protection outside the brain

  • Kill switch at −30 % from peak blocks new entries, and can sell everything
  • Circuit breaker after 3 failed transactions; no trading on a stale feed
  • A buy is refused if its quote is over 3 % above the paper fill; entries pause when live trails paper
  • The signer simulates every swap and caps buys at 1 SOL each, 10 SOL a day, 6 swaps a minute
Status · 2026-10-18

fly-trader is an experiment. Since 2026-10-17 the fly has traded small live wallets on both chains, beside paper books that take every one of its decisions; the live record is days old and proves nothing yet. Installed from source it signs nothing until you turn live trading on; the Docker release trades its funded wallet after 20 closed paper trades, with caps sized for 0.5 SOL. Every observation, decision and fill is written to Postgres. Read the code before you trust it, and never fund it with money you cannot lose.

04 · Get Started

Run your own fly

The whole system is open source under the MIT license: the connectome build, data ingest, training, the trading engine, the signer and the operator console. It runs on a Mac, on a Linux PC with an NVIDIA or AMD GPU, or on any 64-bit CPU, Windows included through Docker.

DEVICE=auto picks a CUDA or ROCm GPU, then Metal, then the CPU; a missing backend falls back to the CPU with a warning. DEVICE=cpu, cuda:1, rocm or mps choose one.

Training from scratch64 GB minimumOptional, 4 GB or moreAbout 0.5 TB, after downloading 1.8 TB of compressed archive (one to two days). The project trains on a 128 GB machine.

Memory decides how fast the fly runs, not whether it runs. Every batch is sized from free memory: about 9.5 MB per row to score and 40 MB per row to train, plus 0.5 GB fixed, within 60 % of what is free. A busy minute (about 140 tokens) scores in under 2 GB. An 8 GB card trains about 100 rows at a time and scores about 470; full-speed batches want about 37 GB free. The selector's walk-forward is what needs the RAM: it keeps every row of the archive in memory (36 million for the current model), and a recent run held 43 GB. On a shared GPU or an integrated one, DEVICE_MEMORY_GB sets how much the fly may use.

You will need

  • Docker, or Python 3.13 and uvDocker runs everything, Postgres included, and keeps itself up to date. From source, uv installs the rest.
  • PostgreSQL 15 or newerFrom source only. Homebrew's works.
  • Jupiter and Helius API keysJupiter for token stats and Solana swaps, Helius for the wallet's transactions. Helius's free tier is enough. Market data needs no key.
  • An Envio HyperSync token (for Robinhood Chain)Personal accounts get one free. Robinhood Chain is off in Docker and on from source; without a token, set RH_ENABLED=0 and trade Solana only.
  • Money you can loseDocker's caps are sized for a 0.5 SOL wallet. From source, nothing is signed until you set LIVE_ENABLED=1.
  • PatienceA fresh install needs a day of market data before it scores anything, and a from-scratch training run takes days of downloading and hours of training.

Install with Docker: trade with the shipped fly

  1. Download the release and add your keys

    The distribution branch carries the trading engine, the console, fly #157, the selector that taught it and their connectome.

    git clone -b distribution https://github.com/bryceweiner/fly-trader.git && cd fly-trader
cp .env.example .env    # paste HELIUS_API_KEY and JUPITER_API_KEY

  2. Start it

    The first start builds the image, creates the database, downloads a 300 MB data table and starts the market feed and the trading engine.

    docker compose up -d --build
# NVIDIA:  docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f docker-compose.cuda.yml up -d --build
# AMD:     docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f docker-compose.rocm.yml up -d --build

  3. Create and fund the wallet

    wallet new prints the bot wallet's address and writes its key into .env; back that file up, it is the only copy. Send it 0.5 SOL, then restart.

    docker compose exec fly fly-trader wallet new
docker compose restart fly

  4. Watch it

    Open http://localhost:8501. The first day is a warm-up; then it trades paper until 20 trades have closed, then the wallet. Its kill switch sells everything 20 % below the wallet's peak. Every hour it installs any newer release signed by the project's key; AUTO_UPDATE=0 pins it.

Install from source: train your own

  1. Clone and install

    On Linux, name a torch build: plain uv sync there installs PyPI's 3 GB CUDA 13 build.

    git clone https://github.com/bryceweiner/fly-trader.git && cd fly-trader
uv sync                  # macOS: Metal
uv sync --extra cu126    # Linux, one of: cpu · cu126 · cu130 (NVIDIA) · rocm72 (AMD)

  2. Configure and create the database

    Copy the template and paste your keys. Leave LIVE_ENABLED=0; paper books run by default.

    cp .env.example .env
createdb fly_trader
uv run fly-trader init-db

  3. Build the brain

    Fetch the public FlyWire export (about 85 MB: three tables from flybrain and the FlyWire annotations), then build. The build checks it against the published 139,255 neurons and 2,698,236 connections.

    mkdir -p data/brain/raw && cd data/brain/raw
F=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snedea/flybrain/main/data
curl -LO $F/connections.csv.gz -LO $F/neurons.csv.gz -LO $F/classification.csv.gz
curl -LO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/flyconnectome/flywire_annotations/main/supplemental_files/Supplemental_file1_neuron_annotations.tsv
cd - && uv run fly-trader build-connectome

  4. Open the console

    One process starts and supervises every worker (the market feeds, the history archive and its features, the trainer, the trading engine) and serves the operator dashboard on http://localhost:8501. It listens on 127.0.0.1 only; reach a remote one through an SSH tunnel.

    uv run fly-trader ui

  5. Train, then trade on paper

    The trainer starts by itself once every archived day has its features, then reruns every 7 days. By hand:

    uv run fly-trader train-selector       # walk-forward backtest over the whole archive
uv run fly-trader train-fly-selector   # teaches the fly from the selector
uv run fly-trader fly-replay           # the replay the fly must pass before it trades

  6. Go live only if you mean it

    Create the bot wallet, fund it modestly and run the smoke test, then set LIVE_ENABLED=1 with your capital caps. Robinhood Chain has its own key (RH_BOT_PRIVATE_KEY), switch (RH_LIVE_ENABLED) and smoke test. Every rail above is enforced in code, and the kill switch is yours.

    uv run fly-trader wallet new
uv run fly-trader swap-smoke --sol 0.01
uv run fly-trader rh-smoke --yes
