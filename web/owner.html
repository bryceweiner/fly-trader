Treasury owner

The fly's SOL lives in a Squads multisig that only your wallet controls. The server holds two keys that may take SOL out of it only through two spending limits: L1 refills the trading wallet itself (per day), L2 pays holders' claims (per week). Revoke both and the server can move nothing out of the treasury, whatever it runs.

Emergency

If you think the server is compromised: revoke both limits now. Trading keeps only what is already in its float; claims wait until you add L2 again.

Setup

Paste the lines fly-trader vault squads-setup prints on the server. Nothing here is sent anywhere but the Solana network; the values stay in this browser.
